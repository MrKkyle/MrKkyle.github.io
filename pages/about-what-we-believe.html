What We Believe

A summary of the biblical truths we teach and practice.

God’s Word Is Our Foundation

We believe the Bible is the inspired and trustworthy Word of God, and our final authority for faith and life. Our teaching, worship, and discipleship are grounded in Scripture.

Jesus Christ Is Lord and Saviour

We believe salvation is by grace alone through faith in Jesus Christ. His life, death, resurrection, and promised return are central to our message and mission.

Called to Holy Living and Mission

We believe God calls believers to prayer, fellowship, and Spirit-led service. As a church, we seek to grow as disciples and share the gospel faithfully in our community and beyond.

Our desire is to glorify God in every ministry by teaching biblical truth and helping people follow Christ in everyday life.
