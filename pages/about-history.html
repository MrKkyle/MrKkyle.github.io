History

How God has guided our church over the years.

Small Beginnings in Faith

Montrose Park Bible Church began as a small group of believers meeting with a shared desire for faithful worship, prayer, and Bible teaching. Those early gatherings laid a strong spiritual foundation built on Scripture and Christ-centered fellowship.

Growing Through the Word

As the church family grew, ministry expanded into discipleship classes, youth support, and community outreach. Week by week, God used faithful preaching and pastoral care to strengthen families and deepen commitment to His Word.

Continuing the Mission Today

Today we continue that same mission: making disciples, serving our city, and proclaiming the gospel with truth and compassion. We remain grateful for God’s faithfulness through every season.

We praise God for every generation that has helped shape this church family, and we look ahead with hope as we continue serving Christ together.
