Ladies Ministry

Encouraging women to flourish in Christ through fellowship, Bible studies, and prayer.

Ladies Ministry provides a warm and supportive space for women of all ages to grow spiritually, build lasting friendships, and serve others. Our ladies gather once per month for fellowship, prayer and Bible study.

Join us as we encourage one another to flourish in Christ, deepen our understanding of God's Word, and live out our faith together.
