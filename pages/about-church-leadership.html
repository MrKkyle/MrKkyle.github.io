Church Leadership

Faithful servants guiding, equipping, and caring for our church family.

Elders

Our church is shepherded by 3 Elders who provide pastoral oversight, guard sound doctrine, and prayerfully lead the congregation in worship, discipleship, and care.

Deacons

We are supported by 2 Deacons who serve faithfully in practical ministry, helping meet needs with compassion so that the wider church body can flourish together.

Men’s Ministry Leader

Our 1 Men’s Leader encourages men to grow in Christ through Bible study, accountability, mentorship, and committed prayer.

Ladies Ministry Leader

Our 1 Ladies Leader helps nurture faith and fellowship among women through teaching, encouragement, and supportive Christ-centered community.

Youth Leaders

The next generation is guided by 3 Youth Leaders who invest in young people through Bible teaching, discipleship, encouragement, and godly example.

Leadership Team Overview

We thank God for each leader who serves with humility, integrity, and a heart for people. Our current leadership structure includes:

  • 3 Elders
  • 2 Deacons
  • 1 Men’s Leader
  • 1 Ladies Leader
  • 3 Youth Leaders

Together, these leaders help guide worship, discipleship, pastoral care, outreach, and ministry development across the church.
