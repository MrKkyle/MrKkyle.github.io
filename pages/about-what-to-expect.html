What to Expect

Helpful details for your first visit.

→ What style are the worship services?

Our services are reverently led and Christ-centred. You can expect congregational hymn singing, Scripture readings, and expository preaching. We aim to honour God and edify all who attend.

→ How long do the services last?

A typical Sunday service runs approximately 90 minutes, including worship, prayer, and a Bible message. You are always welcome to stay afterwards for tea and fellowship.

→ What should I wear?

There is no dress code. Come as you are — some members dress formally while others come more casually. What matters most is your heart, not your attire.

→ What happens when I arrive?

A greeter will welcome you at the door and show you to your seat. We have a comfortable and friendly atmosphere, so feel free to ask any questions or let us know if you need assistance.

→ Will I be expected to give money?

Not at all. Giving is an act of worship for our church members. As a visitor, you are our guest and there is absolutely no expectation or obligation to give.

→ Is there something for my children?

Yes! We have Sunday School classes for children of different age groups running after the main service, normally starting around 11:00 - 12:00. Our teachers use engaging, Bible-based lessons to help kids learn about their saviour Jesus.
