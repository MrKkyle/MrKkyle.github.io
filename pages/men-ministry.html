Men Ministry

Building men of prayer, character, and faithful service in Christ.

Men Ministry equips men to lead faithfully at home, in church, and in the workplace through Bible study, accountability, and fellowship. We encourage practical growth in prayer, integrity, and discipleship.

Activities include monthly Mens meeting with topical Bible studies. Come join us as we grow together in Christ and support one another in our walk of faith.
