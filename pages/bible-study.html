Midweek Bible Study

Gathering each week to study Scripture deeply, pray together, and grow in faith.

Midweek Bible Study is a dedicated time for verse-by-verse learning, thoughtful discussion, and practical application. It is open to adults and young adults seeking deeper understanding of God’s Word.

Sessions include prayer, teaching, and fellowship designed to strengthen everyday discipleship.
