Introduction

Welcome to our church family and mission.

Montrose Park Bible Church is a Christ-centered community committed to worship, Scripture, prayer, and discipleship. We welcome individuals and families from every background to grow in faith together, and to serve our community and the world with the love of Christ.

We are a bible church, falling under no denominational affiliation, that is purely committed to the authority of Scripture, the centrality of Christ, and the power of the Holy Spirit. Please explore our website to learn more about our beliefs, ministries, and how to get involved. We look forward to welcoming you!
